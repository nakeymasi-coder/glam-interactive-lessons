03 / Create your mockup

Take your design into ChatGPT

Open a new ChatGPT chat. Copy the prompt below, paste it into the message box, and send it. Answer one question at a time.

Open ChatGPT

When ChatGPT creates your image, check the wording and layout. Ask for one correction at a time. Save the approved image. If image generation is unavailable on your account, ask for an image-generation prompt instead.

04 / Apply the design

One screen at a time

A picture does not make working buttons. This assistant helps you create or update the actual screen while keeping existing features.

Open ChatGPT

Upload your saved mockup to that chat when asked. ChatGPT will need your actual project files or access to a coding workspace to modify an existing app. Login, payments, databases, and publishing need their own setup; a mockup does not provide them.
