Start with one useful job

Your generator should help one kind of person complete one clear task. Keep your first build small.

Too broad

“A generator for everything in my business.”

Ready to plan

“A generator that helps craft sellers write a friendly product description.”

Pick a starting idea or write your own:

Design three useful choices

Each choice is something the user selects or types. Its value should appear in the finished prompt. Three choices are enough for your first generator.

Example

Choice: Tone
Options: Friendly, professional, playful
Prompt instruction: “Use a friendly tone.”

Use commas to separate options. If a choice needs typed information, write “Custom text” instead.

Connect choices to instructions

A dropdown alone does not make a useful generator. Its selection needs to change an instruction. Tap each choice to see its place in your planned prompt.
