Lesson 1 · What is a prompt generator? · About 15–20 minutes, plus your real-life practice.

Your first small win

By the end, you can choose details, create a prompt, and explain where to use it. No coding experience needed.

A prompt is an instruction you give a tool such as ChatGPT. A prompt generator puts that instruction together from your choices and details.

Your task
What do you want help doing?
Your details
What should the tool know or work around?
Your format
How should the answer look?

Those pieces become one prompt. The generator in this lesson assembles text; it does not create an AI answer. You will copy your prompt into ChatGPT to get the answer.

What does “HTML generator” mean?

HTML organizes a webpage. CSS controls its appearance. JavaScript makes choices and buttons work. These can work together to create a generator you open in a browser. You do not need to memorize these terms today.

Your real-life assignment

  1. Create a prompt in the practice lab for something you actually need.
  2. Copy it, open ChatGPT, paste it into a new conversation, and send it.
  3. Read the answer. Does it follow your details? Check anything important before using it.
  4. If needed, change one instruction and try again.
  5. Return here and record what you learned.
